Practical perspectives

Useful questions for important career and hiring decisions.

Good judgment begins with better questions. These frameworks help you think beyond quick impressions and identify what needs further exploration.

FOR CANDIDATES

What impact can you demonstrate?

Think about the scale of responsibilities, decisions you owned and what changed because of your work. Concrete outcomes are more persuasive than broad adjectives.

FOR EMPLOYERS

What does success look like after a year?

Defining first-year priorities helps interviewers evaluate relevant experience and helps candidates assess whether they can deliver on expectations.

FOR EVERYONE

What remains uncertain?

Make assumptions visible. Clarify work expectations, decision authority, stakeholders, timelines and any constraints before making a major commitment.
