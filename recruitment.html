Search methodology

A rigorous brief makes a sharper search.

Our approach starts with the work a leader must do—not only the title on an organization chart.

DISCOVERY

Translate priorities into a mandate

Define business objectives, reporting relationships, stakeholder dynamics, geographic requirements, leadership capabilities and the decisions the incoming leader will be expected to make.

MARKET

Research relevant talent pools

Consider direct-industry leaders and adjacent-sector candidates whose experience may transfer. Understand availability, motivations and the market realities that affect engagement.

EVALUATION

Develop a defensible shortlist

Use consistent criteria, structured conversations, and evidence of results to assess relevant capabilities. Share meaningful observations and any open questions with the hiring decision-makers.

Search partnership

Considerations beyond a résumé.

Senior appointments affect how an organization executes strategy, manages change and engages its people. Effective hiring processes take into account operating scale, change leadership, cross-functional influence and the environment into which the leader will step.

We approach these factors with a practical focus on role relevance and transparent communication. Final appointment decisions, terms and employer requirements always remain with the hiring organization.

Typical areas of focus

  • Executive and functional leadership appointments
  • Confidential or sensitive leadership transitions
  • Cross-industry and transformation expertise
  • Role definition and interview scorecards
  • Stakeholder alignment and candidate communications
