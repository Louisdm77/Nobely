What to review before applying

A job title never tells the whole story. Use these checkpoints to evaluate whether an opportunity makes sense for you.

01

Role scope and impact

Look closely at who the role reports to, the responsibilities it owns, the team it supports, and the measures of success. An attractive title may represent very different levels of responsibility across organizations.

02

Work model and location

Remote positions can still have regional restrictions, travel requirements, or expected office visits. Review the official listing for eligibility and operational expectations.

03

Qualifications and growth

Compare required experience and tools with your background, then consider where the role could take you. Transferable capabilities can matter, but employers determine their own requirements.
